## 🎨 Visual Style Guide

### Color Palette
- **Primary**: `#667eea` (Purple)
- **Secondary**: `#f093fb` (Pink)
- **Accent**: `#4facfe` (Blue)
- **Background**: Dark gradient (#0a0a0a to #1a1a2e)
- **Text**: White (#ffffff)

### Typography
- **Headings**: Poppins Bold (700)
- **Body**: Inter Regular (400)
- **Accent**: Poppins SemiBold (600)

### Animation Style
- **Easing**: Ease-out, bounce
- **Speed**: Fast transitions (0.3-0.5s)
- **Effects**: Glassmorphism, particles, gradients

---

## 🎵 Audio Elements

### Background Music
- **Style**: Tech-inspired, upbeat
- **BPM**: 120-130
- **Instruments**: Synth, electronic beats
- **Volume**: 30% (background level)

### Sound Effects
- Logo appear: Whoosh + chime
- Check marks: Soft pop
- UI elements: Subtle click
- Button hover: Light beep

### Voiceover
- **Gender**: Professional (male or female)
- **Accent**: Neutral/American
- **Tone**: Confident, friendly
- **Speed**: Moderate (not too fast)
