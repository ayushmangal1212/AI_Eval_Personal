❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How many times can I take an evaluation?
Admins configure default attempts per role; some users may have specific overrides. Check your dashboard or contact an administrator for your exact limit.
Are the evaluations timed?
Yes — questions and full evaluations have configured time limits. You will see countdown timers during the assessment.
Can I use voice input for answers?
Yes — the platform supports speech-to-text for answers and text-to-speech for question playback where available.
How is my score calculated?
AI evaluates each answer on a rubric and assigns a score; the platform aggregates those into a final percentage with per-question feedback.
Who can I contact for help?
Use the support contact provided by your organization or email the admin address configured in the app. Admins can also view logs and feedback from the admin dashboard.
